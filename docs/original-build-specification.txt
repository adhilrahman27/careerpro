# 8. AI JOB MATCHING SYSTEM

Build an explainable personalized job-matching engine.

The engine must compare the candidate profile and available job requirements.

### Matching factors

Use an initial configurable scoring model such as:

- Skills compatibility: 30%
- Job title relevance: 25%
- Experience compatibility: 15%
- Location and work preferences: 10%
- Salary compatibility: 10%
- Employment preferences and recency: 10%

Treat these weights as defaults, not scientifically validated probabilities.

Support hard filters for clearly incompatible requirements.

Handle missing data explicitly and avoid treating unknown information as either a confirmed match or mismatch.

### Results

Show a match score and explanation:

**Example**

Match Score: 87/100

Strong matches:
- Primavera P6
- Construction scheduling
- MEP coordination
- UAE project experience

Potential gaps:
- Power BI reporting
- Specific project sector exposure

Recommendations:
- Highlight relevant scheduling experience in the CV
- Add verifiable project achievements
- Prepare examples of delay analysis

The score represents estimated relevance, **not the probability of receiving an interview or job offer**.

Allow users to understand and modify their preferences.

Provide an AI-disabled fallback matching system.

Cache results appropriately and recalculate when profiles or jobs change.
